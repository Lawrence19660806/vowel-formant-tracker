How to use
  1. Allow microphone access, then pick Men or Women as your reference (default: Men).
  2. Produce one vowel continuously and steadily (e.g. /i/ as in heed, /ɑ/ as in hod, /u/ as in who'd).
  3. The rose tongue shows your live tongue position; aim at its blue target dot.
  4. The faint trail shows your recent attempts (a dot every 1.5 s) — a tight cluster means you are consistent.
  5. Hit ▶ Play back to hear the last 3 seconds of your voice and compare it to the target sound.
